About SOMDA Week

A week where student work took the stage.

SOMDA Week 2026 was an annual showcase organized by the School of Multimedia and Digital Arts (SoMDA) at Mapúa University’s Makati Campus to celebrate student creativity, innovation, and media production.

Spearheaded by the Hiraya SoMDA Student Council alongside various creative clusters within the school, the week-long event featured screenings, interactive showcases, and competitions designed to develop students’ creative skills. It provided a platform for aspiring filmmakers, graphic designers, animators, and artists to share original projects, build their portfolios, and gain recognition among fellow creatives.

The event also strengthened the community among students and faculty while celebrating artistic excellence and a shared passion for digital media.
